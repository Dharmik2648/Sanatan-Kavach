Accessibility & Announcements

A comfortable darshan for everyone.

Mandir Kavach makes accessibility clear before you travel, so every devotee — including elders and differently-abled visitors — can plan with confidence.

Wheelchair Assistance

Free wheelchairs and volunteer support at the Main Gate on request.

Senior Citizen Priority

A dedicated, less crowded East Corridor queue for elders and families with infants.

Medical Room

First-aid and a rest area beside the East Corridor, staffed through darshan hours.

Family &amp; Women Queue

A separate, calmer lane so families can move together safely.
